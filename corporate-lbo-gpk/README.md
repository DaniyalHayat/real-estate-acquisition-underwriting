# GPK Public-to-Private | Private Equity Underwriting

**Independent educational project | October 2026 | Source: Graphic Packaging Holding Company (NYSE: GPK) FY2025 Form 10-K.**

This is a distinct corporate LBO case hosted in a subdirectory of an existing investment-underwriting repository. It is **not real estate underwriting**.

## The investment decision
**Defer / do not approve** a 6.25x FY2025 unadjusted EBITDA purchase of GPK funded at 4.25x initial leverage until capex normalization, debt financing and downside execution have been validated. No actual transaction or investment committee submission occurred.

## Scope and integrity

Reported 2023–2025 sales, EBIT, D&A, net income, cash flow and capex anchor an analyst-assumption 2026–2030 operating / debt cash-flow model. The reported FY2025 unadjusted EBITDA is calculated as EBIT plus D&A, and is **not** management-adjusted EBITDA. This project includes scenario return attribution, downside equity wipeout, an illustrative covenant stress and a simplified 9.5%-WACC / 2.0%-terminal-growth DCF. It **does not** include a full balance-sheet-integrated three-statement forecast, live equity comps, actual takeover terms, underwritten financing quotations or sourced precedent transactions. The 6.25x entry is a chosen valuation assumption, not a market quote.
